Sampling distribution of x̄ if H₀ is true — drag the x̄ slider and watch the shaded p-value

The curve is the world where H₀ is true. The shaded tail is the probability of seeing a sample mean at least this far out in that world — that is all a p-value is. It is not the probability that H₀ is true.

The p-value answers one question only: how surprising is this sample if H₀ were true? It is not the probability that H₀ is true — and nothing special happens at 0.05.
