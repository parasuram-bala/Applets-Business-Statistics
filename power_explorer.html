Sampling distribution of the observed difference — under H₀ (no effect) and under the true effect δ

Type I error (α): H₀ true, we still reject Type II error (β): effect real, we miss it Power (1−β): effect real, we detect it

The two curves are two possible worlds. The dashed line is your decision rule. Moving α, n, or δ just moves how the two worlds fall on either side of it.

Power is the chance your test detects an effect that is really there. Underpowered tests miss real effects — "not significant" is not the same as "no effect."
